
The corpus of Basque simplified texts compiles 227 original sentences of science popularisation domain and two simplified versions of each sentence. The simplified versions have been created following different approaches: the structural  and the intuitive. The sentences are divided in three texts (Bernoulli, Etxeko and Exoplanetak) and have been aligned to their respective simplified versions. The files follow the folowing scheme:

Where XXX means text name  (Bernoulli, Etxeko and Exoplanetak),

XXX_str.txt  -> original and simplified according to the structural aproach (aligned)
XXX_int.txt -> original and simplified according to the intuitive aproach (aligned)

File with .ann extensions contain the annotations.

The annotation scheme we have created to annotate the corpus is divided into eight macro-operations: delete, merge, split, transformation, insert, reordering, no operation and other. This scheme is based on the work by Brunato et al. (2015). It is included in the annotation.conf file. 

In order to see the annotatations, the BRAT rapid annotation tool (Stenetorp et al., 2012) needs to be installed: https://brat.nlplab.org/
